Kerry Bread



Ingredients:

  • 2 tablespoons active dry yeast
  • 5 cups warm water, divided
  • 1 cup cooking oil
  • 2/3 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 tablespoons salt
  • 12 cups all-purpose flour

Instructions:

  1. In a small bowl, dissolve the yeast in 1 cup of warm water.
  2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the remaining 4 cups of warm water, oil, sugar, and salt.
  3. Stir in 6 cups of flour and mix thoroughly.
  4. Add the yeast mixture and stir to combine.
  5. Add the remaining 6 cups of flour and knead the dough for 10 minutes.
  6. Cover and let the dough rise until doubled in size.

To Make Fried Bread:

  • Roll out the dough and cut into squares.
  • Slit the center of each square.
  • Lightly oil a pan and heat over medium heat.
  • Fry the dough squares until dark golden brown on both sides.

To Make Loaves:

  • Form the dough into loaves and place in greased loaf pans.
  • Let rise until doubled again, about 20–30 minutes.
  • Bake at 400°F for 30 minutes.
