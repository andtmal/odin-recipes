Crazy Cake



Ingredients:

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 6 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 12 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons vinegar
  • 2 cups cold water

Instructions:

  1. Sift the dry ingredients (flour, sugar, baking soda, salt, and cocoa powder) into an ungreased 9x13-inch baking dish.
  2. In the dry mixture, make three wells:
    • Into well #1, pour the cooking oil.
    • Into well #2, add the vanilla extract.
    • Into well #3, add the vinegar.
  3. Pour the cold water over the entire mixture.
  4. Stir back and forth with a fork until well blended.
  5. Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes, or until done in the center.
  6. Top with powdered sugar or butter icing while still warm.
